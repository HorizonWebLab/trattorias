About Us

Casual, cozy & romantic sea-facing dining.

Our Story

Trattorias Restaurant is a popular sea-facing rooftop restaurant located at Black Beach, North End Cliff in Varkala, within the Thiruvananthapuram district.

Offering an eclectic menu paired with panoramic ocean and sunset views, Trattorias provides a casual, cozy, and romantic atmosphere perfect for unwinding by the waves.

Ambiance & Atmosphere

Casual, cozy, and romantic rooftop seating overlooking the ocean, making it an idyllic spot to catch spectacular coastal sunsets.
